## The question

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (often called Mormons) teaches that the true church disappeared after the apostles, and that God restored it through **Joseph Smith** (1805–1844), who received new scripture: the _Book of Mormon_, the _Doctrine and Covenants_ and the _Pearl of Great Price_. This page tests those claims against the Bible, history, archaeology and the Church's own publications — including the _Gospel Topics_ essays the Church published on its website in 2013–2014.

Many Latter-day Saints are kind, moral and devoted people. The question is not whether they are sincere, but whether Joseph Smith was a true prophet. The Book of Mormon itself was brought, Smith said, by an angel (Moroni). Paul answered that possibility in advance:

> **Galatians 1:8:** "But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach any other gospel unto you than that which we have preached unto you, let him be accursed."

> **Deuteronomy 18:21-22:** "And if thou say in thine heart, How shall we know the word which the LORD hath not spoken? When a prophet speaketh in the name of the LORD, if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that is the thing which the LORD hath not spoken, but the prophet hath spoken it presumptuously: thou shalt not be afraid of him."

# 1. A living prophet who overrides the dead ones

The Church is led by a president who is sustained as "prophet, seer and revelator." Like the Pope, his word can change what earlier leaders taught. President Ezra Taft Benson put it plainly in "Fourteen Fundamentals in Following the Prophet" (1980): "**The living prophet is more vital to us than the standard works**" and "**the living prophet is more important to us than a dead prophet**."

History shows why that is needed:

- **Polygamy** was revealed as an everlasting covenant (Doctrine and Covenants 132) — then abandoned in 1890 (the Manifesto), when the U.S. government was confiscating Church property and Utah wanted statehood.
- **Black men were barred from the priesthood** and Black members from temple ordinances from 1852 (Brigham Young) until 1978. The Church now disavows the reasons its leaders gave ("Race and the Priesthood," 2013).
- **Brigham Young** taught that Adam "is our Father and our God, and the only God with whom we have to do" (_Journal of Discourses_ 1:50, 1852). President Spencer W. Kimball later condemned this "Adam-God" doctrine as false (October 1976 General Conference).

A God who says "I change not" (Malachi 3:6) does not reverse Himself every few decades. A message that has to be corrected by each new leader is not "the faith which was once delivered unto the saints."

> **Malachi 3:6:** "For I am the LORD, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed."

> **Jude 1:3:** "Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints."

# 2. A different God and a different Jesus

### God was once a man

Joseph Smith taught in his King Follett sermon (April 1844): "**God himself was once as we are now, and is an exalted man**, and sits enthroned in yonder heavens!" President Lorenzo Snow summed it up: "As man now is, God once was; as God now is, man may be." The Father "has a body of flesh and bones as tangible as man's" (Doctrine and Covenants 130:22).

The Bible says the opposite:

> **Numbers 23:19:** "God is not a man, that he should lie; neither the son of man, that he should repent: hath he said, and shall he not do it? or hath he spoken, and shall he not make it good?"

> **John 4:24:** "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."

> **Isaiah 43:10:** "Ye are my witnesses, saith the LORD, and my servant whom I have chosen: that ye may know and believe me, and understand that I am he: before me there was no God formed, neither shall there be after me."

### Three Gods

Two months before his death, Smith declared: "I have always declared God to be a distinct personage, Jesus Christ a separate and distinct personage from God the Father, and that the Holy Ghost was a distinct personage and a Spirit: and **these three constitute three distinct personages and three Gods**" (_History of the Church_ 6:474). He called them "God the first, the Creator; God the second, the Redeemer; and God the third, the witness or Testator" (_Teachings of the Prophet Joseph Smith_, p. 190).

The Bible knows one God, with no other God formed before or after Him — and all the fullness of the Godhead dwells bodily in Jesus.

> **Isaiah 44:6:** "Thus saith the LORD the King of Israel, and his redeemer the LORD of hosts; I am the first, and I am the last; and beside me there is no God."

> **Isaiah 44:8:** "Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee from that time, and have declared it? ye are even my witnesses. Is there a God beside me? yea, there is no God; I know not any."

> **Colossians 2:9:** "For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily."

Strikingly, the **1830 Book of Mormon** was much closer to this. It said "the virgin which thou seest, is **the mother of God**" (1 Nephi 11:18) and "Behold the Lamb of God, yea, **even the Eternal Father**!" (1 Nephi 11:21). In 1837 these were changed to "the mother of **the Son of** God" and "even **the Son of** the Eternal Father." Mosiah 15:1-4 still teaches that God himself would come down as "the Father and the Son… one God." Smith's own scripture was edited to fit his later theology.

### How Jesus was conceived

The Bible says Mary conceived by the Holy Ghost, as a virgin (Matthew 1:18-20; Luke 1:35). Several leading Latter-day Saint authorities taught a physical conception by the Father:

- Brigham Young: "The birth of the Saviour was as natural as the births of our children; **it was the result of natural action**. He partook of flesh and blood — was begotten of his Father, as we were of our fathers" (_Journal of Discourses_ 8:115, 1860).
- Joseph Fielding Smith (later Church president): "Christ was begotten of God. **He was not born without the aid of man, and that man was God!**" (_Doctrines of Salvation_ 1:18).
- Bruce R. McConkie (apostle): "Christ was begotten by an Immortal Father **in the same way that mortal men are begotten by mortal fathers**" (_Mormon Doctrine_, 1966, p. 547).

Heber C. Kimball and Orson Pratt taught the same. The Church today does not officially explain how the conception happened, but it has never repudiated these statements.

> **Luke 1:34-35:** "Then said Mary unto the angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not a man? And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."

### Jesus and Lucifer, brothers

In Latter-day Saint teaching, every person lived before birth as a spirit child of Heavenly Father and a Heavenly Mother. Jesus was the firstborn of these spirit children, and **Lucifer was another**; in the pre-mortal council, the two presented competing plans, and Lucifer rebelled when the Father chose Jesus' (Moses 4:1-4; Abraham 3:27-28). So Jesus, Lucifer and every human being are spirit siblings.

The Bible says Jesus is not one of the created beings but their Creator — including every angel, "thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers."

> **John 1:3:** "All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made."

> **Colossians 1:16-17:** "For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist."

# 3. Joseph Smith: the man and his visions

### The First Vision: alone, and told differently

Smith said that in 1820, praying alone in the woods near Palmyra, New York, he saw God the Father and Jesus Christ in bodily form, who told him that all the churches were wrong. There were no witnesses — and Smith himself told the story in different ways. In his earliest account, written in his own hand in 1832, he was "in the 16th year of my age" and saw only "**the Lord**"; an 1835 account mentions "many angels"; the official 1838 version, written 18 years after the event, has him at 14 seeing two personages, the Father and the Son. The Church acknowledges these differences ("First Vision Accounts," 2013).

### Seer stones and treasure digging

Before the Book of Mormon, Smith hired himself out to find buried treasure by looking at a "seer stone" in a hat. In 1826 he was brought to court in Bainbridge, New York, as "**Joseph Smith the Glass looker**." The Church now confirms that he "translated" the Book of Mormon the same way: he "placed the seer stone in a hat, pressing his face into the hat to block out extraneous light, and read aloud the English words" — the gold plates were often not even in the room ("Book of Mormon Translation," 2013; the stone was photographed in the _Ensign_, October 2015).

### Polygamy and polyandry

The Church admits that Joseph Smith married many women: "Careful estimates put the number **between 30 and 40**." Some were already married to other living men, and one, Helen Mar Kimball, was married to him "several months before her 15th birthday" ("Plural Marriage in Kirtland and Nauvoo," 2014). Doctrine and Covenants 132 threatens his wife Emma with destruction if she did not accept it (132:54).

Meanwhile, he publicly denied it. In May 1844 he said: "**What a thing it is for a man to be accused of committing adultery, and having seven wives, when I can only find one**" (_History of the Church_ 6:411). When a Nauvoo newspaper, the _Expositor_, exposed his polygamy in June 1844, Smith as mayor had its press destroyed — the act that led to his arrest and his death at Carthage jail.

> **Matthew 7:15-16:** "Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves. Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?"

> **1 Timothy 3:2:** "A bishop then must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;"

### Prophecies that failed

The Bible's test is simple: if what a prophet says in the Lord's name does not come to pass, the Lord has not spoken it (Deuteronomy 18:22).

- In September 1832 Smith announced that a temple would be built in **Independence, Missouri**: "which temple shall be reared **in this generation**. For verily this generation shall not all pass away until an house shall be built unto the Lord" (Doctrine and Covenants 84:4-5). Everyone of that generation is dead; the temple was never built.
- In February 1835 he said that "**fifty-six years should wind up the scene**" — the coming of the Lord (_History of the Church_ 2:182). 1891 came and went.

### The Book of Abraham

In 1835 Smith bought Egyptian papyri from a traveling mummy exhibit and "translated" them as the _Book of Abraham_, now scripture in the _Pearl of Great Price_. In 1966 fragments of those very papyri were rediscovered. Egyptologists — including Latter-day Saint scholars — identify them as ordinary Egyptian funerary texts (the _Book of Breathings_ for a man named Hor), written about 1,700 years after Abraham. The Church concedes that "**none of the characters on the papyrus fragments mentioned Abraham's name or any of the events recorded in the book of Abraham**" ("Translation and Historicity of the Book of Abraham," 2014).

# 4. The Book of Mormon

Smith called it "**the most correct of any book on earth**" (_History of the Church_ 4:461, 1841). It tells of Lehi, who left Jerusalem about 600 BC and sailed to the Americas; his descendants divided into Nephites and Lamanites; Jesus appeared to them after His resurrection (3 Nephi 11); and about AD 421 the Lamanites destroyed the Nephites. An earlier people, the Jaredites, are said to have come from the Tower of Babel (the Book of Ether).

### Thousands of changes

Comparing the 1830 first edition with modern editions, researchers Jerald and Sandra Tanner counted **3,913 changes**. Most correct spelling and grammar, but some change doctrine (1 Nephi 11, above), and some fix errors: Mosiah 21:28 said king **Benjamin** when Benjamin was already dead (changed to "Mosiah" in 1837); 2 Nephi 30:6 promised that converted Lamanites would become "**a white and a delightsome people**" (changed to "pure" in 1840, back to "white," then "pure" again in 1981).

### Copied from the King James Version

The Book of Mormon claims to translate plates written 600 BC–AD 421, yet long sections reproduce the **1611 King James Version word for word** — including words that the KJV translators added in italics and passages that were not in the Bible until centuries later:

- Isaiah 2–14 and 48–49 (2 Nephi 12–24; 1 Nephi 20–21).
- The Sermon on the Mount (3 Nephi 12–14) and Malachi 3–4 (3 Nephi 24–25).
- Mormon 9:22-24 quotes the long ending of Mark (Mark 16:15-18), which is missing from the oldest Greek manuscripts.
- Jesus calls Himself "**Alpha and Omega**" (3 Nephi 9:18) — Greek letters — to a people who supposedly spoke a form of Hebrew and wrote "reformed Egyptian," and the names "Jesus" and "Christ" are revealed to Nephi around 545 BC (2 Nephi 10:3; 25:19).

### Things that did not exist in ancient America

- **Horses and chariots:** "he is feeding thy horses… prepare his horses and chariots" (Alma 18:9-10, about 90 BC); also 1 Nephi 18:25, 3 Nephi 3:22. Horses died out in the Americas about 10,000 years ago and were brought back by the Spanish in the 1500s; no wheeled vehicles were used for transport in the pre-Columbian Americas.
- **Elephants, cattle, oxen, cows** (Ether 9:18-19, about 2000 BC). Mammoths and mastodons were extinct in the Americas thousands of years earlier; cattle came with the Europeans.
- **Steel:** the Nephites worked "iron… copper… brass… steel" (2 Nephi 5:15); the Jaredites "made swords out of steel" (Ether 7:9). No steel has ever been found in pre-Columbian America.
- **Wheat, barley and silk** (Mosiah 9:9; Alma 1:29), and a monetary system of gold and silver pieces (Alma 11:4-19): no Nephite money of any kind has ever been found.

### No archaeology, and DNA against it

The Book of Mormon describes great cities, wars in which millions (Ether 15:2) and hundreds of thousands (Mormon 6) were killed, temples, highways and metal weapons. Not one Book of Mormon city, person, king or inscription has ever been identified. The Smithsonian Institution has stated that it has never used the Book of Mormon as a scientific guide and found no direct connection between New World archaeology and its subject matter.

Compare the Bible, whose people and places keep turning up: the "House of David" inscription at Tel Dan (1993); the Pilate Stone at Caesarea (1961); the ossuary of the high priest Caiaphas (1990); the Pool of Siloam (2004); and thousands of coins of the Herods, Pilate and Caesar — the "tribute penny" Jesus held. There are no Nephite coins.

DNA studies show that Native Americans descend from peoples of Asia, not of Israel. The Church's 1981 introduction said the Lamanites "are the **principal ancestors** of the American Indians"; in 2006 it was changed to "**among the ancestors**" ("Book of Mormon and DNA Studies," 2014).

### The witnesses

The Book of Mormon opens with the testimony of "Three Witnesses" and "Eight Witnesses" who saw the plates. But Martin Harris later said he saw them "with spiritual eyes," and all three of the Three Witnesses — Oliver Cowdery, David Whitmer and Martin Harris — broke with Smith and were excommunicated or left the church in 1838; Smith wrote that such men were "too mean to mention" (_History of the Church_ 3:232). Compare the apostles, who saw the risen Jesus with their eyes, ate with Him, and died rather than deny it (see _Evidence for Jesus_).

# 5. Salvation: grace "after all we can do"

The Book of Mormon says: "it is by grace that we are saved, **after all we can do**" (2 Nephi 25:23). Latter-day Saint teaching divides the afterlife into three kingdoms of glory (Doctrine and Covenants 76). **Exaltation** — living with the Father in the highest level of the celestial kingdom and becoming gods, with "a continuation of the seeds forever and ever" (D&C 132:19-20) — requires baptism, the temple endowment, eternal marriage sealed in the temple (D&C 131:1-4), and faithfulness to the end.

To enter the temple, a member must hold a **temple recommend**, which requires, among other things, paying a **full tithe** — a tenth of one's income — and obeying the Word of Wisdom. The Church is very wealthy: in 2023 the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission fined the Church and its investment firm Ensign Peak Advisors $5 million for hiding the size of its stock holdings (about $32 billion) behind shell companies; a 2019 whistleblower put the fund at about $100 billion.

The gospel the apostles preached is different: salvation is a free gift, received by faith, not wages earned by works; and Jesus said that in the resurrection "they neither marry, nor are given in marriage."

> **Ephesians 2:8-9:** "For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast."

> **Titus 3:5:** "Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;"

> **Romans 11:6:** "And if by grace, then is it no more of works: otherwise grace is no more grace. But if it be of works, then is it no more grace: otherwise work is no more work."

> **Matthew 22:30:** "For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven."

# Conclusion

Mormonism shares the Bible's vocabulary — Jesus, grace, atonement, prophets — but fills those words with a different meaning: a God who was once a man, three separate Gods, a Jesus who is Lucifer's brother, and salvation completed by temple works. It rests on one man's vision, told several ways, with no witnesses; on scripture "translated" with a treasure-digging stone in a hat, copied from the King James Version and changed thousands of times; on prophecies that failed; and on a history of ancient America that archaeology and DNA have not found.

The Bible does not need a restoration. Its message was delivered once for all, and its God does not change:

> **Isaiah 45:5:** "I am the LORD, and there is none else, there is no God beside me: I girded thee, though thou hast not known me:"

> **1 Timothy 2:5:** "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus;"

> **1 John 5:20:** "And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true, and we are in him that is true, even in his Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life."
