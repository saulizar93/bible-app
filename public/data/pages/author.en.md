## Saul Ojeda

I am a software developer by profession and a student of God's Word by conviction. What started as a hobby — reading the Bible for myself — became years of study of the Scriptures in their original languages, of church history, and of the translations that stand between the reader and the text. This app is the result.

- **Military service:** Army medic, veteran.
- **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Bioengineering · Master's degree in Cyber Security · Master's degree in Computer Science.
- **Theological training:** I did not attend seminary. Instead, I read the books that seminaries assign — the lexicons, the grammars, the church fathers, the councils and the histories — and checked them against the source documents.

> "Self-education is, I firmly believe, the only kind of education there is." — Isaac Asimov

Scripture itself is not impressed by credentials. When the rulers of Israel saw the boldness of Peter and John, "and perceived that they were **unlearned and ignorant men**, they marvelled; and they took knowledge of them, that **they had been with Jesus**" (Acts 4:13). And the Bereans were called noble not because of their schooling, but because they "**searched the scriptures daily**, whether those things were so" (Acts 17:11).

# Why study this topic?

Salvation is the most important subject there is, so I will not let anyone else tell me how to find it. Men lie. Men seek control. Men work to advance their own institutions. So I went to the source documents myself — and what I found was a web of lies and manipulation running through religion and politics alike. Many of the institutions that claim to speak for God today were shaped by some of the most ruthless political maneuvering in history, and their doctrine bears the marks of it. Jesus warned about exactly this: "in vain they do worship me, **teaching for doctrines the commandments of men**" (Matthew 15:9).

That is why the work of reformation never ends. We must keep returning to the Word because we keep forgetting it. God's instruction to Joshua still stands: "This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but **thou shalt meditate therein day and night**, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein" (Joshua 1:8). And the psalmist tells us why: "**Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee**" (Psalm 119:11).

# Discernment

People who do not read cannot have discernment. Without knowing what the text actually says, there is no way to know who is telling the truth and who is not. Such people often fall back on their feelings — "follow your heart" — but Scripture is clear about the heart: "**The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?**" (Jeremiah 17:9). "There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death" (Proverbs 14:12). And so they fall victim to men who use religion to control others.

What I have learned in my studies is this: **discernment is not the ability to tell truth from a lie. It is the ability to tell the truth from an almost-truth.** Outright lies are easy to reject. The danger is the doctrine that is ninety percent Scripture and ten percent tradition, the translation that changes a single word, the argument that sounds biblical but rests on a council rather than on the text. That is why we are told to "prove all things; hold fast that which is good" (1 Thessalonians 5:21), and to "try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world" (1 John 4:1).

# My goals for this app

1. **Give Spanish speakers the same resources available in English** — parallel Bibles, Strong's numbers, Greek and Hebrew lexicons and study notes in their own language.
2. **Provide historical and theological context** so readers can understand the Bible as its first readers did, and see how and why later traditions departed from it.
3. **Help Oneness Christians defend their faith** against the strongest arguments of Trinitarians and Catholics — not against caricatures, but against their best case.
4. **Expose the faulty reasoning of Trinitarianism**, testing its claims against the plain words of Scripture and the original languages.
5. **Expose the irresponsible use of Scripture in Catholic theology** — mistranslations, proof texts taken out of context, and doctrines built on the Latin rather than on the Hebrew and Greek.
6. **Leave my research behind for my son**, so that he can begin his own study of the Scriptures from a higher starting point than I did — as Timothy, who "from a child… hast known the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus" (2 Timothy 3:15).

"Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth" (2 Timothy 2:15).
