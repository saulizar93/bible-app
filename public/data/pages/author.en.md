## Saul Ojeda

![Saul Ojeda](images/author.jpg)

I am just a regular guy who does not put his trust in men, especially when it comes to salvation. I may not have a seminary degree, but I have read the seminary material. As an introvert, I manage to make time for it (I read or listen to about 50 books a year). I love reading the same topic through the lens of different groups (Catholics, Evangelicals, Presbyterians, Pentecostals, etc.). When you do that, you realize that most people lie by omission. They don't have to utter an actual lie, they just have to omit crucial information that would expose a weakness in their doctrinal stance. My aim for this app is for it to be so thorough that it can defend itself against the best arguments out there even after I am not around to respond. I am trying to build a one-stop site that has it all, in order to quickly answer any objections I may run into. This dream will not be completed until I finish the Study Notes for the entire Bible. I have only completed the book of Matthew so far, but I have tried to be thorough enough that, by simply reading the Matthew Study Notes, the reader would have enough information to easily digest the rest of Scripture.

I am a software developer by profession, but in my free time I love to read Scripture and test my understanding of it on _Threads_ (@saulojeda). If my views can survive scrutiny, then I know I am on the right track. So if I open my mouth, it is because I am confident I can defend my position against the best arguments out there (my arguments here are not made public for the first time, they've endured heavy scrutiny on _Threads_ against educated people and against AI). I would not be able to look at myself in the mirror if I tried to defend the Trinity using argurments like "eternally begotten," "Jesus is the Father of eternity only," "1 + 1 + 1 = 1," or changing the defintion of "God" mid-sentence to make my theology fit.

# Why study this topic?

Salvation is the most important subject there is, so I will not let anyone else tell me how to find it. Men lie. Men seek control. Men work to advance their own institutions. So I went to the source documents myself — and what I found was a web of lies and manipulation running through religion and politics alike. Many of the institutions that claim to speak for God today were shaped by some of the most ruthless political maneuvering in history, and their doctrine bears the marks of it. Jesus warned about exactly this: "in vain they do worship me, **teaching for doctrines the commandments of men**" (Matthew 15:9).

Christianity is believing what Christ taught as recorded in Scripture. Religiosity is believing what men say God says.

> “Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch” (Matthew 15:14).

The work of reformation never ends. We must keep returning to the Word because we keep forgetting it. God's instruction to Joshua still stands: "This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but **thou shalt meditate therein day and night**, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein" (Joshua 1:8). And the psalmist tells us why: "**Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee**" (Psalm 119:11).

# Discernment

People who do not read cannot have discernment. Without knowing what the text actually says, there is no way to know who is telling the truth and who is not. Such people often fall back on their feelings — "follow your heart" — but Scripture is clear about the heart:

> "**The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?**" (Jeremiah 17:9).

> "There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death" (Proverbs 14:12).

> “Jesus answered and said unto them, **Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures**, nor the power of God.” (Matthew 22:29).

And so they fall victim to men who use religion to control others.

What I have learned in my studies is that **discernment is not the ability to tell truth from a lie, but the ability to tell the truth from an almost-truth.** Outright lies are easy to reject. The danger is the doctrine that is ninety percent Scripture and ten percent tradition of men: like a translation that changes a single word, or late source information taken as apostolic. That is why we are told to "prove all things; hold fast that which is good" (1 Thessalonians 5:21), and to "try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world" (1 John 4:1).

# My goals for this app

1. **Give Spanish speakers the same resources available in English** — parallel Bibles, Strong's numbers, Greek and Hebrew lexicons and study notes in their own language.

2. **Provide historical and theological context** so readers can understand the Bible as its first readers did, and see how and why later traditions departed from it.

3. **Help Oneness Christians defend their faith** against the strongest arguments of Trinitarians and Catholics.

4. **Expose the faulty reasoning of Trinitarianism**, testing its claims against the plain words of Scripture and the original languages.

5. **Expose the irresponsible use of Scripture in Catholic theology** — mistranslations, proof texts taken out of context, and doctrines built on the late Latin rather than on the original Hebrew and Greek.

6. **Leave my research behind for my son**, so he can begin his own spiritual journey from a higher standard, and not just from empty, baseless, human tradition.

> “My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children” (Hosea 4:6).

> "And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge" (2 Peter 1:5).
