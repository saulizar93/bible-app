These questions are meant for honest, respectful conversation with Trinitarian friends. Each one comes from a passage of Scripture that is hard to fit into the doctrine of "one God in three co-equal, co-eternal persons," but fits naturally if there is **one God, who revealed Himself as Father, in the Son, and as the Holy Spirit**, and whose name is **Jesus**. Read the verses under each question first; the question only asks what the verses themselves raise.

Verses are quoted from the **King James Version (KJV)**, which follows the Textus Receptus. When a question depends on a reading found in the Greek manuscripts used by modern critical editions, the verse is quoted from the **Berean Standard Bible (BSB)**; when it depends on the reading of the majority of Greek manuscripts, from the **Majority Standard Bible (MSB)**.

For the history of how the doctrine developed, see the page **Development of the Trinity**.

# 1. The Name and baptism

### 1. Why did the apostles always baptize in the name of Jesus?

Not once in the New Testament does anyone baptize by saying "in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost." Every baptism that names a formula names Jesus. Believers were baptized "calling on the name of the Lord," and Paul implies that the Corinthians were baptized in the name of the One who was crucified for them.

> **Acts 2:38 (KJV):** "Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost."

> **Acts 8:16 (KJV):** "(For as yet he was fallen upon none of them: only they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus.)"

> **Acts 10:48 (KJV):** "And he commanded them to be baptized in the name of the Lord. Then prayed they him to tarry certain days."

> **Acts 22:16 (KJV):** "And now why tarriest thou? arise, and be baptized, and wash away thy sins, calling on the name of the Lord."

> **1 Corinthians 1:13 (KJV):** "Is Christ divided? was Paul crucified for you? or were ye baptized in the name of Paul?"

> **Romans 6:3-4 (KJV):** "Know ye not, that so many of us as were baptized into Jesus Christ were baptized into his death? Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life."

> **Galatians 3:27 (KJV):** "For as many of you as have been baptized into Christ have put on Christ."

> **Colossians 2:12 (KJV):** "Buried with him in baptism, wherein also ye are risen with him through the faith of the operation of God, who hath raised him from the dead."

### 2. Why did Paul rebaptize the disciples at Ephesus?

They had already been baptized by immersion, for repentance, by John's baptism. Yet when Paul heard it, they were baptized again "in the name of the Lord Jesus." If the name spoken at baptism did not matter, why baptize them again?

> **Acts 19:1-5 (KJV):** "And it came to pass, that, while Apollos was at Corinth, Paul having passed through the upper coasts came to Ephesus: and finding certain disciples, He said unto them, Have ye received the Holy Ghost since ye believed? And they said unto him, We have not so much as heard whether there be any Holy Ghost. And he said unto them, Unto what then were ye baptized? And they said, Unto John’s baptism. Then said Paul, John verily baptized with the baptism of repentance, saying unto the people, that they should believe on him which should come after him, that is, on Christ Jesus. When they heard this, they were baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus."

### 3. In Matthew 28:19 Jesus said to baptize in "the name" — singular — of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost. What is that one name?

Jesus did not say "the names." Scripture tells us there is only one name given among men by which we must be saved.

> **Matthew 28:19 (KJV):** "Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:"

> **Acts 4:10 (KJV):** "Be it known unto you all, and to all the people of Israel, that by the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, whom ye crucified, whom God raised from the dead, even by him doth this man stand here before you whole."

> **Acts 4:12 (KJV):** "Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved."

### 4. Did Peter disobey Jesus?

Peter heard the command of Matthew 28:19 with his own ears. A few weeks later, on the day of Pentecost, he commanded the crowd to be baptized "in the name of Jesus Christ." Luke's account of the same commission says that repentance and remission of sins would be preached "in his name… beginning at Jerusalem." Did Peter misunderstand, or did he understand exactly what the name was?

> **Luke 24:47 (KJV):** "And that repentance and remission of sins should be preached in his name among all nations, beginning at Jerusalem."

> **Acts 2:38 (KJV):** "Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost."

> **Mark 16:15-17 (KJV):** "And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature. He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned. And these signs shall follow them that believe; In my name shall they cast out devils; they shall speak with new tongues;"

### 5. In Matthew 28:20 Jesus said, "Lo, I am with you alway." How, if He was about to ascend to heaven? Isn't it the Holy Spirit who stays with us forever?

> **Matthew 28:20 (KJV):** "Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen."

> **John 14:16-18 (KJV):** "And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever; Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you. I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you."

### 6. What is the name of the Father?

Jesus said He came in His Father's name, that He manifested the Father's name, and that He declared it.

> **John 5:43 (KJV):** "I am come in my Father’s name, and ye receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him ye will receive."

> **John 17:6 (KJV):** "I have manifested thy name unto the men which thou gavest me out of the world: thine they were, and thou gavest them me; and they have kept thy word."

> **John 17:26 (KJV):** "And I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it: that the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them."

> **Hebrews 2:12 (KJV):** "Saying, I will declare thy name unto my brethren, in the midst of the church will I sing praise unto thee."

### 7. What is the name of the Holy Spirit?

Jesus said the Father would send the Holy Ghost "in my name."

> **John 14:26 (KJV):** "But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you."

### 8. What is the name of the Son, and what does it mean?

The angel gave the name, and explained it: "for he shall save his people from their sins." In Hebrew, Yeshua means "YHWH is salvation." Yet God says that beside Him there is no saviour.

> **Matthew 1:21 (KJV):** "And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins."

> **Isaiah 43:11 (KJV):** "I, even I, am the LORD; and beside me there is no saviour."

### 9. If the Son "inherited" His name, from whom did He inherit it?

A son inherits his father's name. The name of Jesus is "above every name," and the majority of Greek manuscripts call the Father's name "the name which you gave me."

> **Hebrews 1:4 (KJV):** "Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they."

> **Philippians 2:9-11 (KJV):** "Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name: That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth; And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father."

> **John 17:11 (MSB):** "I will no longer be in the world, but they are in the world, and I am coming to You. Holy Father, protect them by Your name, the name You gave Me, so that they may be one as We are one."

### 10. If there is one LORD and His name is one, how many names do Father, Son and Holy Spirit have?

> **Zechariah 14:9 (KJV):** "And the LORD shall be king over all the earth: in that day shall there be one LORD, and his name one."

> **Deuteronomy 6:4 (KJV):** "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD:"

### 11. In eternity, God and the Lamb have one throne, one face and one name. Whose name will be on our foreheads?

> **Revelation 22:3-4 (KJV):** "And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him: And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads."

> **Revelation 14:1 (MSB):** "Then I looked and saw the Lamb standing on Mount Zion, and with Him 144,000 who had His name and His Father’s name written on their foreheads."

# 2. What does "God" mean?

### 12. In John 1:1, what does "God" mean each time it appears?

"In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God." If "God" means the Trinity, then the Word was with the Trinity and was the Trinity. If the first "God" means the Father, then the Word was the Father. Trinitarians answer that the first "God" means the Father and the second means the divine nature — so the meaning of the word changes in the middle of the same sentence. Is that reading the text, or reading the doctrine into it? Read plainly, God's Word was with Him and was Himself, and that Word "was made flesh."

> **John 1:1 (KJV):** "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."

> **John 1:14 (KJV):** "And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth."

### 13. In 1 Timothy 2:5, who is the "one God," and between whom does the mediator stand?

"For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus." If the "one God" is the Trinity, then God the Son is mediating between the Trinity — which includes Himself — and men. If the "one God" is the Father only, then the Son is not part of the "one God." Either way, the mediator is identified as "the man Christ Jesus." Isn't Paul describing the one God and the humanity in which He reconciles us?

> **1 Timothy 2:5 (KJV):** "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus;"

### 14. In John 4:24, who is the Spirit?

"God is a Spirit." If "God" here means the Trinity, then the Trinity is a Spirit — so is the Holy Spirit one person inside a Spirit? If "God" means the Father, then the Father is a Spirit and the Holy Spirit is another Spirit — but Scripture says there is one Spirit. Isn't it simpler that the one God is Spirit, and that "the Holy Spirit" is that same God at work?

> **John 4:24 (KJV):** "God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth."

> **Ephesians 4:4 (KJV):** "There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling;"

### 15. In 1 Corinthians 8:6, if the "one God" is the Father, where are "God the Son" and "God the Holy Spirit"?

And if Jesus Christ is the "one Lord," is the Father not Lord? Paul is quoting the Shema of Deuteronomy 6:4, "The LORD our God is one LORD" — and he never adds "in three persons."

> **1 Corinthians 8:6 (KJV):** "But to us there is but one God, the Father, of whom are all things, and we in him; and one Lord Jesus Christ, by whom are all things, and we by him."

> **Deuteronomy 6:4 (KJV):** "Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God is one LORD:"

### 16. When asked for the greatest commandment, why did Jesus quote "The Lord our God is one Lord" and never mention three persons?

> **Mark 12:29 (KJV):** "And Jesus answered him, The first of all the commandments is, Hear, O Israel; The Lord our God is one Lord:"

### 17. In John 17:3, if the Father is "the only true God," what is the Son?

> **John 17:3 (KJV):** "And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent."

### 18. In John 14:1, Jesus said, "ye believe in God, believe also in me." If "God" means the triune God, who already includes the Son, is Jesus asking for faith in a fourth person?

> **John 14:1 (KJV):** "Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me."

### 19. In 2 Corinthians 5:19, which God was "in Christ"?

"God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself." Was the Trinity in Christ? Was the Father in the Son? Notice "unto himself": the God who was in Christ reconciled the world to Himself.

> **2 Corinthians 5:19 (KJV):** "To wit, that God was in Christ, reconciling the world unto himself, not imputing their trespasses unto them; and hath committed unto us the word of reconciliation."

> **Colossians 2:9 (KJV):** "For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily."

### 20. In Acts 20:28, whose blood purchased the church?

"The church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood." The Father has no blood, the Spirit has no blood, and the Trinity has no blood. Whose blood is it, if not the blood of the one God "manifest in the flesh"?

> **Acts 20:28 (KJV):** "Take heed therefore unto yourselves, and to all the flock, over the which the Holy Ghost hath made you overseers, to feed the church of God, which he hath purchased with his own blood."

> **1 Timothy 3:16 (KJV):** "And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory."

### 21. Hebrews 1:3 calls the Son "the express image of his person" — singular. If God is three persons, which person is Jesus the image of?

> **Hebrews 1:3 (KJV):** "Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;"

> **Colossians 1:15 (KJV):** "Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature:"

### 22. In Genesis 1:26-27, if "Let us make man in our image" means three persons, why does the next verse say God created man "in his own image"?

> **Genesis 1:26-27 (KJV):** "And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth. So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them."

### 23. Even 1 John 5:7 in the KJV says "these three are one." One what? The verse does not say "three persons in one being." Doesn't "one" fit better with one God bearing witness as Father, Word and Holy Ghost?

> **1 John 5:7 (KJV):** "For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one."

# 3. Co-equal?

### 24. If the Father and the Son are co-equal, why did Jesus pray to the Father for help and submit His will to the Father's will?

> **Matthew 26:39 (KJV):** "And he went a little further, and fell on his face, and prayed, saying, O my Father, if it be possible, let this cup pass from me: nevertheless not as I will, but as thou wilt."

### 25. If they are co-equal, why did Jesus say, "my Father is greater than I"?

> **John 14:28 (KJV):** "Ye have heard how I said unto you, I go away, and come again unto you. If ye loved me, ye would rejoice, because I said, I go unto the Father: for my Father is greater than I."

### 26. Why did Jesus say He could do nothing of Himself and sought the will of the Father? Isn't God the Son almighty?

> **John 5:30 (KJV):** "I can of mine own self do nothing: as I hear, I judge: and my judgment is just; because I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me."

### 27. Why did Jesus say He did not speak of Himself, but what the Father "commanded" Him? Do co-equal persons command one another?

> **John 12:49 (KJV):** "For I have not spoken of myself; but the Father which sent me, he gave me a commandment, what I should say, and what I should speak."

> **John 8:28 (KJV):** "Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things."

### 28. Why did Jesus say that the words He spoke were not His own, and that "the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works"? Is God the Father inside God the Son?

> **John 14:10 (KJV):** "Believest thou not that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? the words that I speak unto you I speak not of myself: but the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works."

### 29. Why did the Son not know the day or the hour, but only the Father? If they share one divine nature, don't they share the same knowledge?

> **Mark 13:32 (KJV):** "But of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father."

### 30. Why did Jesus say, "Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God"? Is Jesus not good? Is He not God?

> **Mark 10:18 (KJV):** "And Jesus said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God."

> **Matthew 19:17 (KJV):** "And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God: but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments."

### 31. Why did Jesus say, "He that believeth on me, believeth not on me, but on him that sent me"? If He is God the Son, why redirect faith away from Himself?

> **John 12:44-45 (KJV):** "Jesus cried and said, He that believeth on me, believeth not on me, but on him that sent me. And he that seeth me seeth him that sent me."

### 32. Why did Jesus cry, "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?" Can one person of the Trinity forsake another? Can God be separated from God?

> **Matthew 27:46 (KJV):** "And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is to say, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?"

> **Psalm 22:1 (KJV):** "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?"

### 33. In Philippians 2:6-8, who humbled Himself and took the form of a servant: "God the Son," or the one God who came in the flesh?

> **Philippians 2:6-8 (KJV):** "Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God: But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross."

### 34. John 5:23 says all should honour the Son as they honour the Father. Where is the Holy Spirit? Why is there no command to honour the Spirit as the Father?

> **John 5:23 (KJV):** "That all men should honour the Son, even as they honour the Father. He that honoureth not the Son honoureth not the Father which hath sent him."

### 35. Why is blasphemy against the Holy Ghost unforgivable, but a word against the Son can be forgiven? If both are co-equal persons, why the difference?

> **Matthew 12:31-32 (KJV):** "Wherefore I say unto you, All manner of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men: but the blasphemy against the Holy Ghost shall not be forgiven unto men. And whosoever speaketh a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but whosoever speaketh against the Holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this world, neither in the world to come."

### 36. Did "God the Son" die? Scripture says the Son died. Can God die? Can one third of God die?

> **Romans 5:10 (KJV):** "For if, when we were enemies, we were reconciled to God by the death of his Son, much more, being reconciled, we shall be saved by his life."

> **1 Corinthians 15:3 (KJV):** "For I delivered unto you first of all that which I also received, how that Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures;"

# 4. The throne, the right hand, and the kingdom

### 37. If the Son is co-equal and eternal, why will He deliver the kingdom to the Father and be subject to Him, "that God may be all in all"?

> **1 Corinthians 15:24-25 (KJV):** "Then cometh the end, when he shall have delivered up the kingdom to God, even the Father; when he shall have put down all rule and all authority and power. For he must reign, till he hath put all enemies under his feet."

> **1 Corinthians 15:28 (KJV):** "And when all things shall be subdued unto him, then shall the Son also himself be subject unto him that put all things under him, that God may be all in all."

### 38. But Hebrews 1:8 says the Son's throne is "for ever and ever," and Luke says "of his kingdom there shall be no end." Will the Son's reign end or not?

> **Hebrews 1:8 (KJV):** "But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of thy kingdom."

> **Luke 1:32-33 (KJV):** "He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David: And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end."

### 39. If the Son delivers everything to the Father, why does Ephesians say Christ will present the church "to himself"?

> **Ephesians 5:27 (KJV):** "That he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish."

### 40. Why does the Psalm say the Lord would sit at the right hand "until" his enemies are made his footstool? If the Son is eternal, why the "until"?

> **Acts 2:34-35 (KJV):** "For David is not ascended into the heavens: but he saith himself, The LORD said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, Until I make thy foes thy footstool."

### 41. How many will you see on the throne in heaven?

John saw "one" sitting on the throne. In the new Jerusalem there is "the throne of God and of the Lamb," and yet "his servants shall serve him… and his name shall be in their foreheads." Jesus said He sat down with His Father in His throne. Will there be three thrones, three bodies, or one?

> **Revelation 4:2 (KJV):** "And immediately I was in the spirit: and, behold, a throne was set in heaven, and one sat on the throne."

> **Revelation 22:3-4 (KJV):** "And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him: And they shall see his face; and his name shall be in their foreheads."

> **Revelation 3:21 (KJV):** "To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne."

### 42. If God is a Spirit, what is His "right hand"?

Jesus sat down "on the right hand of God," Stephen saw Him "standing on the right hand of God," and John says He is "in the bosom of the Father." A Spirit does not have a literal hand or bosom. And when Stephen saw the glory of God, he called on only one: "Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

> **Mark 16:19 (KJV):** "So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God."

> **Acts 7:55-56 (KJV):** "But he, being full of the Holy Ghost, looked up stedfastly into heaven, and saw the glory of God, and Jesus standing on the right hand of God, And said, Behold, I see the heavens opened, and the Son of man standing on the right hand of God."

> **John 1:18 (KJV):** "No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him."

> **Acts 7:59 (KJV):** "And they stoned Stephen, calling upon God, and saying, Lord Jesus, receive my spirit."

### 43. In Revelation 21, the one sitting on the throne says, "I am Alpha and Omega… I will be his God, and he shall be my son." In Revelation 22, Jesus says, "I am Alpha and Omega." Is the one on the throne the Father of the overcomers, or Jesus?

> **Revelation 21:5-7 (KJV):** "And he that sat upon the throne said, Behold, I make all things new. And he said unto me, Write: for these words are true and faithful. And he said unto me, It is done. I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely. He that overcometh shall inherit all things; and I will be his God, and he shall be my son."

> **Revelation 22:13 (KJV):** "I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, the first and the last."

> **Revelation 22:16 (KJV):** "I Jesus have sent mine angel to testify unto you these things in the churches. I am the root and the offspring of David, and the bright and morning star."

# 5. One Spirit

### 44. Who is the Father of Jesus: God the Father, or the Holy Ghost, by whom Mary conceived?

> **1 John 1:3 (KJV):** "That which we have seen and heard declare we unto you, that ye also may have fellowship with us: and truly our fellowship is with the Father, and with his Son Jesus Christ."

> **Matthew 1:18 (KJV):** "Now the birth of Jesus Christ was on this wise: When as his mother Mary was espoused to Joseph, before they came together, she was found with child of the Holy Ghost."

> **Matthew 1:20 (KJV):** "But while he thought on these things, behold, the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a dream, saying, Joseph, thou son of David, fear not to take unto thee Mary thy wife: for that which is conceived in her is of the Holy Ghost."

> **Luke 1:35 (KJV):** "And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."

### 45. If there is only one Spirit, why does Scripture speak of the Holy Spirit, the Spirit of Christ, the Spirit of your Father and the Spirit of God? Are these four spirits, three, or one?

> **Ephesians 4:4 (KJV):** "There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling;"

> **1 Corinthians 12:13 (KJV):** "For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit."

> **1 Peter 1:11 (KJV):** "Searching what, or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow."

> **Matthew 10:20 (KJV):** "For it is not ye that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you."

> **Romans 8:14 (KJV):** "For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God."

### 46. Why does Acts 16 call the Holy Ghost "the Spirit of Jesus"?

In verse 6 they were forbidden "of the Holy Ghost"; in verse 7, in the critical Greek text and in many translations (the BSB, NASB, ESV, NIV, CSB, ASV, NRSV, NABRE, the Douay-Rheims), "the Spirit of Jesus" did not allow them. The same Spirit is called both.

> **Acts 16:6 (KJV):** "Now when they had gone throughout Phrygia and the region of Galatia, and were forbidden of the Holy Ghost to preach the word in Asia,"

> **Acts 16:7 (BSB):** "And when they came to the border of Mysia, they tried to enter Bithynia, but the Spirit of Jesus would not permit them."

### 47. In Romans 8:9, Paul uses "the Spirit of God" and "the Spirit of Christ" interchangeably. Isn't that the Holy Spirit? Then whose Spirit is the Holy Spirit?

> **Romans 8:9 (KJV):** "But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his."

### 48. Why did God send "the Spirit of his Son" into our hearts? Didn't the Father send the Holy Ghost?

# 9. The begotten Son

### 94. If "God the Son" is eternal, when was He begotten?

Hebrews and Acts say, "**this day** have I begotten thee," and Acts applies it to God raising up Jesus. The angel told Mary that the child would be called the Son of God **because** the Holy Ghost would overshadow her. Paul says God sent His Son "made of a woman." When is that "today"? It does not sound like eternity.

> **Hebrews 1:5 (KJV):** "For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?"

> **Acts 13:33 (KJV):** "God hath fulfilled the same unto us their children, in that he hath raised up Jesus again; as it is also written in the second psalm, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee."

> **Luke 1:35 (KJV):** "And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God."

> **Galatians 4:4 (KJV):** "But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,"

> **John 1:14 (KJV):** "And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth."

> **John 3:16 (KJV):** "For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."

### 95. Where does the Bible say "God the Son," "God the Holy Spirit," "eternal Son," "three persons," or "Trinity"?

The Bible says "the Son of God," again and again. It never once says "God the Son." If the doctrine is essential to salvation, why are its basic terms missing from Scripture?

> **Matthew 16:16 (KJV):** "And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God."

> **John 20:31 (KJV):** "But these are written, that ye might believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God; and that believing ye might have life through his name."

# Conclusion

Each of these passages fits naturally with what Israel confessed and Jesus repeated: "The Lord our God is one Lord." The one God, who is Spirit, was "manifest in the flesh"; the Son is that one God's own human life, in which He prayed, submitted, suffered, died and rose; and the Holy Spirit is that same God dwelling in His people — "the Spirit of Christ," who says, "I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you." And the one name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost is **Jesus**.

"For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily. And ye are complete in him." (Colossians 2:9-10)
